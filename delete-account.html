Delete Your GhorNirman Account

Effective date: 2026-10-13 (previous version: 2026-10-11)

If you would like to delete your GhorNirman account and associated data, follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to ranjan12nov90@gmail.com from any email address, with the subject line "Delete my GhorNirman account".
  2. In the email, include the phone number registered on your GhorNirman account, and whether you signed up as a Buyer, Seller, or Mistri.
  3. We will verify the request and delete your account.

What gets deleted

Once your deletion request is processed:

  • Your phone number and account record are deleted.
  • If you were a Seller, Wholesaler or Mistri, your shop or business profile, listed items (including wholesale items), any photos you uploaded (including your profile photo), and any location you saved are deleted.
  • Your data is removed within 30 days of a verified request.

App usage analytics (see the privacy policy) are not linked to your name or phone number, so they cannot be looked up by account. They are deleted automatically by Google Analytics within 14 months.

We do not retain a backup copy of deleted account data beyond what is needed to complete the deletion process itself.
