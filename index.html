Privacy Policy for GhorNirman

Effective date: 2026-09-17

GhorNirman ("the app", "we", "us") is a marketplace app that helps buyers find construction material sellers and mistris (masons and carpenters) in Assam, and helps sellers and mistris list themselves for buyers to find. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

Phone number. When you sign up or log in, we collect your phone number and verify it using a one-time password (OTP) sent by SMS. This verification is handled by Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google. We use your phone number to:

  • identify your account and let you log back in,
  • let buyers contact sellers/mistris directly by phone call or WhatsApp (a seller's or mistri's phone number is shown to buyers on their profile so buyers can reach them — this is the core purpose of the app),
  • send you notifications about your own account, such as when your seller or mistri listing is approved.

District and location selection. Sellers and mistris choose the district (and, where applicable, shop details) where they operate, so buyers can browse and search for them by area. Buyers select a district to browse listings relevant to them. We do not collect precise GPS location — only the district you select yourself.

Shop and listing information. If you register as a seller or mistri, we collect the information you choose to provide about your shop or your services (such as shop name, items for sale and their prices, or the type of skilled work you offer), so that this information can be shown to buyers.

Photos. If you upload photos (for example, of your shop or of items you sell), those photos are stored so they can be shown to buyers.

How we use this information

We use the information above only to operate the core functions of the app: letting buyers find and compare sellers/mistris, and letting buyers contact them directly. We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see your information

If you register as a seller or mistri, your shop/listing details and phone number become visible to buyers using the app once your account is approved, since the purpose of registering is to be found and contacted by buyers. Buyers' own phone numbers are not shown to sellers or other buyers — only used internally for login.

Data storage

App data is stored using Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Storage), which are Google Cloud services. Data is retained for as long as your account is active.

Your choices

You can stop using the app at any time. If you would like your account and associated data deleted, contact us using the email below and we will remove it.

Children's privacy

GhorNirman is intended for adult buyers, sellers, and tradespeople conducting business, and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above.
