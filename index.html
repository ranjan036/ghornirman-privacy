Privacy Policy for GhorNirman

Effective date: 2026-10-19 (previous version: 2026-10-18)

GhorNirman ("the app", "we", "us") is a marketplace app that helps buyers find construction material sellers and mistris (masons and carpenters) in Assam, and helps sellers and mistris list themselves for buyers to find. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

Phone number. Buyers can use the app without signing in. When a seller or mistri signs up or logs in, we collect their phone number and verify it using a one-time password (OTP) sent by SMS. This verification is handled by Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google. We use your phone number to:

  • identify your account and let you log back in,
  • let buyers contact sellers/mistris directly by phone call or WhatsApp (a seller's or mistri's phone number is shown to buyers on their profile so buyers can reach them — this is the core purpose of the app),
  • send you notifications about your own account, such as when your seller or mistri listing is approved.

District selection. Sellers and mistris choose the district where they operate, so buyers can browse and search for them by area. Buyers choose one or more districts to browse listings relevant to them.

Location (optional). The app uses your device's location only when you tap a location button, and only while the app is open. It never tracks your location in the background.

  • Sellers and mistris can tap "Use my current location" on their profile to save a location (for a seller, the shop; for a mistri, the area where they usually work). This saved location is stored with your profile and is part of your public listing, like your shop address. The app uses it to show buyers how far away you are and to sort nearby results first. For mistris, only an approximate area is saved: the location is rounded to about 1 km before it is stored, so a mistri's exact position (for example, their home) is never saved, and buyers see an approximate distance such as "~2 km away". It is optional: if you do not set it, your listing still appears, just without a distance. You can update it at any time from your profile.
  • Buyers can tap "Near me" to see how far sellers and mistris are and to sort or filter results by distance. Your location is used only on your phone to calculate those distances. It is not sent to or stored on our servers, not shown to anyone, and not recorded in analytics.

You can refuse or later turn off location permission for GhorNirman in your phone's settings. Everything except distance features keeps working.

Shop and listing information. If you register as a seller or mistri, we collect the information you choose to provide about your shop or your services (such as shop name, items for sale and their prices, or the type of skilled work you offer), so that this information can be shown to buyers. Shops may also add a GSTIN and/or trade licence number; if added, it is shown to buyers on the shop's page.

Government ID (mistris and workers). Mistris, electricians, plumbers and daily labourers registering on GhorNirman must add a photo of a government ID card (such as a masked Aadhaar, Voter ID, Driving licence or PAN card) and choose its type, so that we can check who is offering work to buyers. If the photo is of an Aadhaar card, the app automatically hides the first 8 digits of the Aadhaar number and the QR code on your phone, before uploading, so only the last 4 digits ever reach us (app version 0.1.10 and later; on older versions we ask that Aadhaar cards be masked). This masking is done entirely on your phone; the unmasked photo is not uploaded or sent to anyone, and the masking cannot be undone. We do not record the ID number itself. The ID photo is stored privately and is seen only by the GhorNirman team for checking; it is never shown to buyers or other users. Once checked, buyers see only an "ID verified" label on the profile.

Wholesaler information. If you register as a wholesaler or distributor, we collect your business details (business name, owner name, phone and WhatsApp numbers, district and the districts you supply, categories, a GSTIN or trade licence number (at least one is required), optional photo and location) and your wholesale items, prices and minimum order quantities. This information is shown only to shops approved on GhorNirman, not to the public.

Photos. If you upload photos (for example, of your shop or of items you sell), those photos are stored so they can be shown to buyers. Sellers and mistris can also add an optional profile photo, such as a photo of their shop front or of themselves, taken with the phone's camera or chosen from its gallery. The app only uses the camera or gallery when you choose to add a photo. A profile photo is shown to buyers with your listing once it is approved, and you can change or remove it at any time from your profile; a removed or replaced photo is deleted from our storage.

App usage information (analytics). We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used, so we can see which features help people and fix what doesn't work. The app records events such as: a material or mistri search being opened (with the category or skill type and how many districts were chosen), an item search (with the words typed in the search box and how many results were found), a price comparison being opened (with the item name), the "Near me" button or a distance filter being used, a Call, WhatsApp or Place order button being tapped, an order being sent, the WhatsApp message language being changed, and a seller or mistri saving their location. These events do not include your name, phone number, or location coordinates. Item searches are meant for item or material names — please don't type personal details in the search box. Like most apps using this service, Google Analytics also automatically collects basic technical information such as your device model, operating system version, app version, an app-specific identifier, and an approximate region derived from your internet connection. We do not collect your device's advertising ID, and this data is not used for advertising. Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics for no longer than 14 months and is then deleted automatically.

Orders you place. When you use "Place order", the app opens WhatsApp with your order written out for the shop, and you decide whether to send it. The app also saves a record of the order request: the shop, the item, the quantity, the price shown, which screen it came from, the message language and the time. This record does not include your name, phone number or location; the optional name and delivery place you type go only into the WhatsApp message. Order records are seen by the GhorNirman team and used to count orders and improve the app; the shop an order was sent to can also see how many order requests it received (the count only). The message language you choose, and the districts you last searched in, are saved only on your phone.

Calls and WhatsApp messages to shops. When you tap a shop's Call or WhatsApp button, the app saves a small record: which shop, whether it was a call or WhatsApp, which screen it came from, and the time. It does not include your name, phone number or location, and the app does not know whether the call connected or what was said. These records let the shop see how many buyers contacted it through GhorNirman in the last 7 and 30 days (counts only), and are also seen by the GhorNirman team.

Wholesale (B2B) orders. When an approved shop places an order with a wholesaler, the order record described above also notes that it is a wholesale order and which shop placed it (the shop's GhorNirman account). This lets us count wholesale orders between businesses. It is seen only by the GhorNirman team.

How we use this information

We use the information above only to operate and improve the core functions of the app: letting buyers find and compare sellers/mistris, showing how far they are, and letting buyers contact them directly. We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see your information

If you register as a seller or mistri, your shop/listing details, phone number, and (if you set them) your profile photo and saved location become visible to buyers using the app once your account is approved, since the purpose of registering is to be found and contacted by buyers. If you register as a wholesaler, your business details and wholesale prices are visible only to approved shops on GhorNirman, not to the public. Buyers do not sign in, so the app does not collect or show any buyer's name or phone number. Usage analytics are seen only by the GhorNirman team, in aggregate. A shop can see only the number of calls, WhatsApp messages and order requests it received through the app, never who sent them. Government ID photos of mistris and workers are seen only by the GhorNirman team and are never shown to buyers.

Data storage

App data is stored using Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Storage), which are Google Cloud services. Seller and mistri data is retained for as long as the account is active. Analytics data is handled by Google Analytics for Firebase and deleted automatically as described above.

Your choices

You can stop using the app at any time. Location is optional: you can decline the permission, and sellers and mistris can simply not set a location. If you would like your account and associated data (including any saved location) deleted, contact us using the email below and we will remove it. See also our account deletion page.

Children's privacy

GhorNirman is intended for adult buyers, sellers, and tradespeople conducting business, and is not directed at children.
